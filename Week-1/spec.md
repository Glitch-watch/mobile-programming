Expense Tracker with Bill Scanning — Project Overview
Introduction

Managing personal or household finances is a daily challenge that most people struggle to keep up with consistently. While traditional methods of expense tracking — pen-and-paper ledgers or basic spreadsheets — are functional, they demand significant manual effort and discipline, which often leads people to abandon the habit within a few weeks. With smartphones now equipped with high-quality cameras and powerful processing capabilities, there's an opportunity to simplify this process significantly.

This project proposes an Expense Tracking Application that allows users to record their expenses in two ways: through manual data entry for full control and flexibility, and through automated bill/receipt scanning, where the user simply captures a photo of a bill and the system extracts key details (amount, date, vendor, category) automatically using Optical Character Recognition (OCR). This dual approach aims to combine convenience with accuracy, encouraging more consistent financial tracking habits.

Problem Statement
Most individuals find it tedious and time-consuming to log every expense manually, which leads to incomplete or abandoned tracking over time.
Physical bills and receipts are easily lost, faded, or misplaced, making it hard to reference past purchases or verify spending later.
Existing budgeting apps often require extensive manual input for every transaction, offering little to no automation for capturing expense data.
Without a consolidated, accurate, and low-effort tracking system, users struggle to understand their spending patterns, set realistic budgets, or identify areas of overspending.
There is a lack of accessible tools that combine manual flexibility with automated bill capture in one seamless application.
Objectives
Enable manual expense entry — allow users to add expenses with details such as amount, category, date, payment method, and notes.
Enable bill capture via camera — allow users to photograph physical bills/receipts and automatically extract relevant data (amount, vendor, date) using OCR.
Categorize and organize expenses — automatically or manually classify expenses into categories (food, transport, utilities, etc.) for better analysis.
Provide visual insights — generate summaries, charts, and reports (daily/weekly/monthly) to help users understand their spending trends.
Store and manage receipt images — retain scanned bill images alongside transaction records for future reference or proof of purchase.
Ensure data accuracy and easy correction — allow users to review and edit OCR-extracted data before saving, in case of scanning errors.
Support budgeting features — allow users to set spending limits per category and receive alerts when nearing or exceeding them.
Ensure a simple, intuitive user experience — minimize the effort required to log an expense, whether manual or photo-based.
