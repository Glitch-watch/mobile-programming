HOW IT WORKS

Two ways in, one place to see it all

Use whichever fits the moment — a quick manual entry at your desk, or a photo on your way out of a shop.

01

Add it by hand

Enter the amount, pick a category, add a note. Takes about ten seconds and gives you full control over the details.

02

Or snap the receipt

Photograph the bill. Snaptally reads the total, the vendor name, and the date straight off the paper.

03

Check what it found

Scanned details show up ready to save. Fix anything that looks off before it lands in your ledger.

04

Watch the picture form

Every entry, typed or scanned, feeds the same categories, charts, and budgets — no separate lists to reconcile.
